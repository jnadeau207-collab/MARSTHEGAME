# ADR 0023: Invalidate Phase 5 Candidate and Recover Through One Art-Directed Hero Sequence

## Status

Accepted — 2026-07-27

## Context

The previous Phase 5 candidate accumulated real renderer and runtime capabilities: HDR rendering, tone mapping, generated PBR inputs, shadows, environment lighting, atmosphere, temporal processing, particles, an articulated test rig, synthesized audio, capture, and automated performance/image evidence.

The exact native build passed its engineering checks. The founder then directly launched and reviewed it. The visible result remained a primitive technical graybox: arena walls, random rock scatter, a block-based character, weak material identity, broad red tinting, excessive presentation effects, and a giant prototype objective banner.

The discrepancy demonstrated that technical feature presence and numerical image thresholds cannot establish visual quality.

## Decision

1. The previous Phase 5 candidate is invalidated.
2. Its useful native engine and renderer systems are retained.
3. Phase 5 restarts around one original, continuous, tightly scoped hero sequence: **Ares Reach: First Light at Relay 03**.
4. External AAA screenshots and primary production presentations are used to derive general visual principles, with an explicit prohibition on copying protected assets or distinctive designs.
5. The replacement sequence is authored under three pillars: **Human Scale**, **Earnest Frontier**, and **Functional Mars Infrastructure**.
6. The visible block rig, featureless arena walls, giant objective banner, glowing objective pillar, uniform red terrain, and global post-processing veil are binary rejection conditions.
7. Phase 5 requires deterministic H1–H4 captures and founder approval of the exact candidate head.
8. Automated image checks remain regression and integrity checks only.
9. Phase 6 remains blocked until the replacement Phase 5 gate passes.

## Consequences

- Art direction, previsualization, character silhouette, environment composition, material identity, lighting, UI, and audio staging become first-class production work rather than renderer side effects.
- The project accepts replacement of rejected visible content despite sunk implementation cost.
- Scene, camera, material, character, UI, capture, and authoring systems may require structural expansion before detailed polish.
- Scope is reduced to one sequence so quality can be concentrated and judged honestly.
- No future assistant or contributor may infer visual approval from CI, merge status, numerical thresholds, or the presence of rendering features.
