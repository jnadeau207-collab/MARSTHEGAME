# Phase 1 Quality Contract

## Current verdict

- Phase: 1
- Slice: fictionalized Mars landing
- Status: in progress
- AAA-quality claim: **not achieved**
- Human team: one founder
- AI collaboration: ChatGPT

`config/phase1_quality.json` is the machine-readable state. `tools.phase1_quality_audit` fails CI if the repository claims unearned quality or fabricated external evidence.

## First runtime tranche

This tranche establishes shared production systems rather than adding more scene-specific effects:

- normalized accessibility settings,
- deterministic camera look-ahead and seeded shake,
- reduced-motion behavior,
- global presentation cues and cinematic framing,
- accessibility-scaled hit-stop and flashes,
- procedural event-driven sound effects,
- explicit master, music, SFX, ambience, dialogue, and UI buses,
- safe silent fallback when audio hardware is unavailable,
- adaptive audio states derived from production scenes,
- deterministic event evidence for tests and replay diagnostics.

These systems are active in the engine. Menu movement, movement states, damage, pickups, terminals, death, and victory now feed the same presentation and audio contracts.

## What this does not prove

It does not prove:

- a complete Mars-landing level,
- shipping-quality art assets,
- final music or voice production,
- fixed-step simulation,
- checkpoint transactions,
- crash diagnostics,
- renderer readiness,
- gamepad completion parity,
- external completion or emotional-response metrics,
- AAA-quality completion.

Those remain explicit gates rather than implied progress.

## Quality doctrine

Scope may be reduced before standards are reduced. A feature that cannot meet the slice bar is cut, redesigned, or delayed. Documentation, budget language, and ambition never substitute for direct play, measurements, and external evidence.
